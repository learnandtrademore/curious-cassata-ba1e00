History of
Govt. Model High School, Khudda Jassu, U.T., Chandigarh

Government Model High School Khudda Jassu is a prestigious co-educational School under the department of education UT Chandigarh Administration. Initially there was only Anganwadi center in Khudda Jassu. School was started in the Dharamshala of village Khudda Jassu in 1992.

In 1995 village panchayat donated the land for the school and 23 rooms were constructed for the school. As a result it was established as a primary school. This school is indulged in providing skill education as there was also a stitching center for women. In 2001 it was upgraded to Middle school.

In 2007 Dispensary and sampark center were added in the school campus. In 2013 it was further upgraded to Secondary School holding its affiliation with CBSE. The school has marked several significant milestones reflecting its commitment to innovation and quality education.

Some of the recent notable developments include introduction of new education policy, smart class rooms and Balvatika classes. The school also introduced skill courses in IT and Apparel which provide students with hands-on training and practical skills in these areas.
